Roach Rush

A cute and funny Python game where you play as a roach on a mission to steal the cake without getting caught!

Game Objective

Your goal is to reach the delicious cake while avoiding the watchful eyes of the guard. Use quick reflexes and stealth to succeed!

Controls

Left Arrow / D Key: Move forward (rush toward the cake)
Spacebar: Play statue (freeze in place when the guard is looking)

Gameplay Mechanics

- Move forward when the guard's eyes are closed
- Press Spacebar to freeze when the guard opens their eyes
- If you move while the guard is watching, you'll get caught and turn into a roach pancake!
- Reach the cake to win the game
-Press R to restart

Artwork

All game visuals are original hand-drawn artwork created specifically for this project.
